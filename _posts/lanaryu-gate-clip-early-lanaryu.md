## Summary

The swinging wooden gate on the Eldin-to-Lanayru road can push Wolf Link through its own collision. This opens Lanayru before the normal Goron Mines progression and is the connection between [Early Master Sword](/posts/early-master-sword) routing and early access to Lakebed Temple.

The clip does not supply the abilities needed to complete the province. Entering without Early Master Sword leaves Link unable to transform into wolf form for the Lanayru twilight insects, creating a progression dead end rather than a useful sequence break.

## Wolf Sidehop Clip

Two Bulblins occupy the setup area. Knock them down or kill them first so their attacks cannot alter the gate cycle or Link's position.

1. Face the gate slightly left of center, with Wolf Link angled only a little to the right of parallel.
2. Hold target and hold left before beginning the attempt.
3. Press `B` into the gate. The bonk starts the gate swinging.
4. Sidehop as the returning gate overlaps Wolf Link. A single swing gives roughly four useful sidehop attempts before settling.

The gate does not need a visibly large swing. Entry is controlled mainly by Link's angle and the sidehop timing. Holding down-left instead of left can make Wolf Link back into the collision; pressing `A` on the earliest post-bonk frame can produce a jump attack instead of a sidehop.

For repeated attempts, backwalk far enough to re-center before bonking again. Starting beyond the centerline makes the sidehop travel away from the clipping seam.

## Dash Variant

The original wolf method used a dash instead of a sidehop. Its successful release is frame-perfect and the working frame can vary, making it a tool-assisted or research setup rather than the normal real-time method.

## Epona Method

An older setup places Epona against the gate at an acute angle, then uses a sidehop or roll so her collision pushes Link through. It is slower to arrange and has been superseded by the wolf sidehop, but remains useful when comparing collision behavior or testing versions.

## Route Consequences

With Early Master Sword, the clip can route around Goron Mines and reach the final Fused Shadow in Lakebed Temple. Without it, early province access does not become early province completion. Save files made in the resulting state should be treated as experimental until a return route is confirmed.
