## Summary

Early Master Sword (EMS) uses a [Wolf Link Super Jump](/posts/wolf-link-super-jump) from a Twilit Messenger in North Faron to reach the out-of-bounds wall leading to Sacred Grove. Link can then complete the Grove and collect the Master Sword during Faron Twilight, far earlier than the story expects.

The reward is larger than the sword itself. Master Sword possession enables free human/wolf transformation outside twilight, Midna warps while human, stronger early combat, and routes into areas whose checks assume those abilities are still unavailable. EMS is therefore a progression state, not merely an early item pickup.

Discovered by Paraxade, SexyZora19, and Kazooie. The faster left-side setup was developed by LogitechSDAZ and Chimpas.

## Portal Fight State

Reach the North Faron Twilit Messenger encounter through normal Faron Twilight movement. Do not finish the portal fight: one of its Messengers is the superjump target.

Tear routing matters. A route that intends to finish Faron Twilight after obtaining the sword must leave the required tears available, then return for them after EMS. Killing or completing the wrong encounter state can remove the enemy needed for another attempt.

## Standard Left-Side Setup

The left/right names follow the established GameCube route notation.

1. Stand by the large tree root and lure the lone Messenger against the outer wall.
2. Begin Midna's charged area attack while maintaining target lock.
3. Move Wolf Link underneath the Messenger so the attack line points into the wall rather than back into the arena.
4. Release during the Messenger's swing. The lunge carries Wolf Link onto the wall collision.
5. Follow the wall toward Sacred Grove until the Grove entrance appears on the minimap.
6. Dash from the wall to the ground in front of the entrance.

Three variables combine at the release: the Messenger must be close enough to the wall, Wolf Link must be on the correct side beneath it, and the swing must be at the usable point in its animation. The Messenger has two swing animations; both can work, but one gives a substantially cleaner release window.

The final drop also needs deliberate speed and angle. Walking off or dashing too shallow can intersect the void before Wolf Link reaches the landing.

## Faster Left-Side Setup

The faster setup performs the same superjump nearer the arena entrance. Place the Messenger flush with the wall and slightly forward of the large root, then bait an attack facing away from the wall. On the slower swing, charge beside it and move around its left before releasing at the standard timing.

Messenger placement is lenient along the wall but not away from it. If a small gap remains, a spin attack can push the enemy flush before the attempt. This correction still saves time over walking to the standard launch point.

## Right-Side Variant

The right-side jump is optimized for bypassing the North Faron portal fight, not for reaching the Master Sword. It reaches the onward route quickly when the portal itself is unnecessary, but takes longer to approach Sacred Grove than either left-side setup.

This distinction matters in categories that use the same collision break without immediately collecting the sword: “EMS jump” can refer to the wall escape even when the route's actual goal is Forest Temple or the remaining insects.

## Progression Fallout

EMS changes assumptions across the early game:

- The normal Goron Mines sumo sequence can become unusable. Routes either clip into the dungeon, trigger the necessary text through [Text Displacement](/posts/text-displacement), or skip the dungeon with [Early Lanayru](/posts/lanaryu-gate-clip-early-lanaryu).
- Early Lakebed reaches stalactites that normally expect the Bow. Those obstacles need an alternate skip or explosive setup.
- Some orderings delay or interfere with the Eldin Bridge warp portal. Blowing up the Lanayru rocks before completing Lakebed preserves a later opportunity to obtain it.
- The new transformation and warp permissions enable secondary glitches including [Golden Wolf Storage](/posts/golden-wolf-storage), Gorge Skip routing, and unusual human-in-twilight states.

A successful sword pickup therefore does not prove the rest of a route is completable. The planned Goron Mines entry, Lakebed traversal, bridge portal, and remaining Faron tears should be decided before committing a save.

## Fixed EMS

The historical “Fixed EMS” route opened the Master Sword chamber but left the sword behind, returned through Sacred Grove, and used a save/void/Game Over sequence to resume normal Goron Mines progression. The sword was collected later by re-entering the Grove with a bomb boost.

Spinner entry and text-displacement solutions made this detour obsolete, but it remains useful evidence that opening the pedestal route and taking the Master Sword are separable progression events.
