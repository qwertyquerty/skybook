## Summary

Death Sword Skip reaches the ledge behind the arena's exit gate without defeating Death Sword. The standard method uses Death Sword itself as the target for a [Wolf Link Super Jump](/posts/wolf-link-super-jump). A Jump Strike [Long Jump Attack](/posts/long-jump-attack-lja) can cross before the fight begins, and a backslice [Moon Boots](/posts/moon-boots) damage boost provides a third, less practical route.

Skipping the fight saves the battle, but does not clear its room state. After collecting the Spinner, normal routes prepare a deathwarp because Death Sword and the locked arena still block the return trip. Health and bomb routing are therefore part of the skip rather than an unrelated optimization.

## Standard Superjump

1. Start the fight and hit Death Sword with the Clawshot or Gale Boomerang so it begins flying.
2. Roll into the corner left of the exit gate and turn the camera perpendicular to the wall.
3. Wait for Death Sword to land nearby and begin a sword swing. If it remains hovering by the gate, use the flying-position backup instead of forcing the normal setup.
4. As the swing begins, roll once away from the corner and transform into Wolf Link. Pan the camera downward during the transformation so the ground spacing remains visible.
5. After the sword strikes the floor, begin holding `B`, then walk roughly nine steps away from the wall.
6. Release the charged attack in the position-specific window. Wolf Link should travel over the gate and land on the upper ledge.

Begin the charge before walking away. Starting both inputs together can move Wolf Link far enough to lose Death Sword as the area-attack target.

The miss direction diagnoses the release:

- A very early release hits Death Sword's sword.
- An early release bonks left of the landing.
- A late release bonks right of the landing.
- A very late release slides along the right wall and may land near the pot.

Depending on Death Sword's position, the successful release window ranges from nonexistent to about five frames. Repositioning the boss is preferable to grinding a launch geometry with no valid frame.

## Jump Strike LJA

The pre-fight method starts from the entrance ledge and never pulls Death Sword's sword.

1. Align Link's left foot with the third vertical floor line from the right.
2. Aim the Gale Boomerang at the upper-right portion of the first pillar across the arena; drawing it nudges Link away from the wall.
3. Set five boomerang targets, with the final target above the high platform.
4. Use the thick vertical wall seam ahead to establish the launch angle.
5. Throw the boomerang and charge Jump Strike.
6. Release while the boomerang is over the high platform, holding up-left through the jump to reach the ledge.

This method replaces enemy manipulation with precise standing position, boomerang path, and launch angle. It is useful when the route already has Jump Strike and wants to avoid starting the encounter.

## Backslice Moon Boots

A third method lets Death Sword's swing damage Link at the peak of a backslice while Moon Boots height is active. The knockback carries Link to the ledge. Its actor placement and timing are tighter than the other variants, and it is slower, so its main value is in restricted or challenge categories.

## Spinner Exit

Once over the gate, continue to the Spinner chest. Because Death Sword was never defeated, plan an exit before committing:

- Standard speed routes lower Link to the required health beforehand, take any final damage in phase one if necessary, collect the Spinner, and deathwarp.
- Extra accidental health can turn a successful gate skip into a trapped or slower state.
- A route that elects to fight Death Sword instead can leave through the cleared room normally.

The time comparison for the skip must include any damage setup needed for that deathwarp, not just the gate crossing.
