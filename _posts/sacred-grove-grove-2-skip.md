## Summary

Grove 2 Skip crosses the high wall near the start of the second Sacred Grove visit. Landing out of bounds gives access to the first Skull Kid arena and bypasses most of the chase, including the second Skull Kid fight.

The modern method combines [Jump Strike Moon Boots](/posts/moon-boots) with a bomb boost. Older superjump and backslice routes establish several other ways across the wall, but their enemy or rupee requirements make them unsuitable for normal runs.

## Route Consequences

Leaving through the first arena avoids the transition that forces daytime. In 100%, this allows the Master Sword pedestal Poe to be collected on the way to Temple of Time instead of requiring a separate warp.

The skip also leaves the pushable block beside Temple of Time in its original position. Any route that returns through that entrance needs another way past the block, such as [Temple of Time Big Key with Jump Strike Moon Boots](/posts/temple-of-time-big-key-with-jump-strike-moon-boots).

## Moon Boots Route

1. Align beside the jagged section of the outer wall. A useful visual reference places the third heart from the right near the wall's horizontal seam, though HUD layout and final facing still matter.
2. Start a jump strike while wearing Iron Boots or heavy Magic Armor.
3. Time a bomb explosion to add horizontal movement as Link rises.
4. Force-unequip the heavy item during the launch so the boosted vertical speed is retained under normal gravity.
5. Land on or beyond the jagged collision and continue out of bounds to the first arena.

The setup has two adjacent two-frame timing cases around the bomb's seventh red flash:

- Releasing jump strike after the flash uses the earliest Moon Boots frame and gives the taller arc.
- Releasing near the peak of the flash uses the following Moon Boots frame and gives a lower arc.

Mixing the later release with the maximum-height unequip is a common failure: Link rises above the explosion's useful range and receives no bomb boost. Correct timing can still fail from a shallow angle, which makes Link slide off the wall instead of clearing it.

## Legacy Routes

The double-backslice method manipulates yellow- and blue-rupee drops and their first-pickup messages to build enough displacement. The drop requirements make it a demonstration route rather than a race setup.

Wolf superjump variants use a puppet enemy to reach the wall. The 100% route must dig immediately after the launch to kill excess momentum, then preserve the required transformation state. Another variant combines the superjump with an LJA and a bomb boost across the Lost Woods loading boundary. Both are substantially less practical than Moon Boots.
